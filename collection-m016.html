IN THE MARGIN

Makhzan al-asrār; Khulāṣat al-khamsah

Verse around verse

Looking at the page

Slanting lines surround two central columns on three sides, with a red passage in the outer writing.

The surrounding writing has its own rhythm and occupies much of the available margin. A margin can hold another text as well as commentary: its position alone does not establish that it explains the central passage.

A question for the reader

What does the note add to the passage beside it—an explanation, a correction, or another reading? Its placement invites that question; establishing its meaning requires a careful transcription.
