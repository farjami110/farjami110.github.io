AI for Reasoning, Norms, and Safety

I am Ali Farjami, a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Luxembourg. I work on Explianable AI with a focus on logical and normative reasoning: how we can build AI systems that reason about obligations, permissions, uncertainty, and provide auditable explanations.

My research interests span several areas:

  • Neuro-symbolic AI: combining neural models with rule-based reasoning
  • Machine ethics & AI safety: normative constraints and alignment-oriented reasoning
  • Logic under uncertainty: modal/deontic formalisms and formal verification
  • Argumentation & informal logic: mining and evaluating reasons in text
