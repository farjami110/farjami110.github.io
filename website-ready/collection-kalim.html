Looking at the page

An illuminated heading above columns of Persian verse, with generous margins around the writing.

The illuminated heading draws the eye toward the verse; the surrounding space gives the writing room to breathe. Here, the margin invites attention through the composition of the page. It offers a visual companion to the practice of annotation explored in In the margin.
