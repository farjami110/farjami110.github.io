IN THE MARGIN

Muntakhab-i Mas̲navī-i sharīf

Marks beside the verse

Looking at the page

Bold markings occupy the right margin beside paired lines of verse; smaller writing appears near the foot of the page.

The contrast between the regular verse and the larger marginal marks makes the surrounding space conspicuous. These marks may record a different kind of engagement from a sustained commentary; their purpose cannot be established from appearance alone.

A question for the reader

What does the note add to the passage beside it—an explanation, a correction, or another reading? Its placement invites that question; establishing its meaning requires a careful transcription.

Source

Digital reproduction. Source file: Muntakhab-i Mas̲navī-i sharīf.pdf; PDF page 25 (scan index, not a folio number).
